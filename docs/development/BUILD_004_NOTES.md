## Core demonstration
A roster can contain enough formally qualified people and still be operationally weak in a critical competency.

This is the bridge from individual training data to an organizational readiness decision.

## Important boundary
All names, scores, thresholds, staffing rules, and procedures are synthetic and illustrative. Mission Readiness is not a validated safety metric.
