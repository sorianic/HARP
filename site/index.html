THE PROBLEM

Autonomy changes where humans enter the system.

As autonomous systems handle more routine operation, human involvement can become less frequent but more consequential. Traditional training records answer whether someone completed training or holds a qualification. HARP explores a different question: how strong is the current evidence that required competencies can still be demonstrated?

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Qualification ≠ readiness.

An operator can remain formally qualified while the evidence supporting a rare, safety-critical competency becomes stale. HARP keeps those concepts separate instead of pretending a course-completion date proves present-day proficiency.

NORTH STAR
“The less frequently humans are required to intervene, the more important it becomes to know they are ready when intervention is necessary.”
