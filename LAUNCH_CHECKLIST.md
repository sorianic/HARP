- [ ] Use the README's 60-second narrative.
- [ ] Keep the concept paper and architecture document visible in `/docs`.
- [ ] Do not describe HARP as production ADS software, a validated safety model, or a regulatory standard.

## Stop rule
After launch defects are fixed, do not add features to v0.1. Capture future ideas separately. The portfolio objective is a clear, credible proof of operational systems thinking, not a production SaaS platform.
